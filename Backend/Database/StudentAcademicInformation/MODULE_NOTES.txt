Two separate academic modules are intentionally preserved.

1) Student Academic Details (existing task)
   GET /api/v1/student-admissions/{admissionId}/academic-details
   PUT /api/v1/student-admissions/{admissionId}/academic-details
   Source table: studentadmissions
   SPs: sp_StudentAdmission_GetAcademicDetails, sp_StudentAdmission_UpdateAcademicDetails

2) Academic Information View/Update (Uday task)
   GET /api/v1/student-academic-information/{academicId}
   PUT /api/v1/student-academic-information/{academicId}
   Source table: student_academic_details
   SPs: sp_StudentAcademicInformation_GetById, sp_StudentAcademicInformation_Update
